# Getting help with MINK

1. **Check the docs.** The [language guide](docs/language/LANGUAGE_BASICS.md), the
   [standard library reference](docs/language/STANDARD_LIBRARY_REFERENCE.md) and the
   [error codes](docs/language/ERROR_SYSTEM.md) answer most questions. `mink explain E-S01` prints the
   explanation of an error from the command line.
2. **Check the known limits.** [docs/KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md](docs/KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md) lists what does not
   work yet and what only looks like a bug on some PCs.
3. **Search the issues.** Someone may have asked already:
   <https://github.com/p4inz-code/mink/issues>
4. **Open a new issue.** Use the bug report form. Include:
   - the output of `mink --version` and your Windows version;
   - the smallest `.mink` file that shows the problem;
   - what you expected and what happened.

Security problems go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public issue.

MINK is made by one person. Replies can take a few days.
