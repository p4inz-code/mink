Rules
  • Use the logo as supplied. Do not stretch, rotate or recolour parts of it, and do not add outlines, shadows, gradients or effects to it.
  • Keep clear space around it equal to the height of the letters.
  • You may say a program is written in MINK or made with MINK, and show the logo for that.
  • Do not use the name or logo so that something looks like the official MINK, or the work of its creator.
  • The logo and name belong to their creator. The Apache License covers the code, not the name or the logo.

The complete brand pack, with every size and colourway, is in assets/brand in the repository.
