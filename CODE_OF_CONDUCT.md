# Code of conduct

MINK is a small project made by people who want to get things working. This page says how we treat each
other while doing that.

## What we expect

- Be kind and be specific. Say what is wrong and what you tried, not who is to blame.
- Assume good faith. Most confusion is a missing explanation, not a bad intention.
- Give credit. Name the person whose idea or fix it was.
- Keep to the topic in issues and pull requests.
- Respect that people have different experience, languages and time zones.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, or promoting unrelated products.
- Passing off someone else's work as your own, including MINK itself. The license requires the credit and
  the NOTICE file to stay with every copy.

## When something goes wrong

Report it to the maintainer through a private GitHub security advisory or by opening an issue that says
you have a conduct report, with no details. The maintainer will answer with a private way to send it.
Reports are read and handled in confidence.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who break these rules.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any space that uses the MINK name, such
as the project's pages and chats.
